## How to Use This Handbook

### **Option 1: Print at Home as Booklet**
1. Open `liver-handbook.pdf`
2. Print with booklet settings (2 pages per sheet)
3. Fold and staple
4. Cost: ~$0.25 per booklet

### **Option 2: Print at Copy Shop**
1. Take PDF to FedEx/Staples
2. Ask for "saddle-stitch booklet, 5.5 × 8.5"
3. 20-50 copies = $20-40
4. Professional quality

### **Option 3: Sell on Amazon (KDP Paperback)**
1. Upload to KDP as paperback
2. Choose 5.5" × 8.5" trim size
