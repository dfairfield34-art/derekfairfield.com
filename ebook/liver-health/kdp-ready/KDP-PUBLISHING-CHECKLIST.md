## 🚀 STEP 1: CREATE YOUR KDP ACCOUNT

1. Go to **https://kdp.amazon.com**
2. Sign in with your Amazon account (or create one)
3. Complete your account profile
4. **Complete tax information:**
   - US authors: Submit W-9 form
   - International authors: Submit W-8 form
   - This is required before you can publish
5. **Set up payment information:**
   - Add your bank account for royalty deposits
   - Set payment threshold (minimum $10)

## 💰 PAGE 3: KINDLE eBOOK PRICING

### **KDP Select Enrollment** (Optional)

**What is KDP Select?**
- Exclusive to Amazon for 90 days
- Get extra royalties from Kindle Unlimited reads
- Access to promotional tools (5 free promo days, Countdown Deals)
- Can't sell your book anywhere else during enrollment

**Recommended for beginners:** ✓ Enroll in KDP Select

**If you want to sell elsewhere:** Leave unchecked

### **Territories**

Select: **"All territories (worldwide rights)"**

OR choose specific countries if you have restrictions

### **Pricing**

**Primary Marketplace: Amazon.com (United States)**

**Royalty Plan:**
Choose: **70% Royalty**

Requirements for 70%:
- Price between $2.99 - $9.99 ✓
- File size under 10 MB ✓
- Enrolled in all territories ✓

**List Price:**
```
$7.99 USD
```

**Why $7.99?**
- Premium positioning for comprehensive guides
- 70% royalty = $5.59 per sale (after delivery costs)
- Higher perceived value
- Still qualifies for 70% royalty

**Alternative pricing:**
- $4.99 - Good entry price, $3.49 per sale
- $9.99 - Maximum for 70% royalty, $6.99 per sale
- $2.99 - Minimum for 70%, $2.09 per sale

### **Pricing for Other Marketplaces**

KDP will auto-populate prices for other countries (UK, Canada, Australia, etc.)

You can adjust these if desired, or leave automatic.

### **Kindle MatchBook** (Optional)

- Leave unchecked (this is for print book buyers)

### **Book Lending**

- **Recommended:** Enable lending
- Allows readers to lend your book to friends (one-time, 14 days)
- Increases word-of-mouth marketing

## ⏰ STEP 4: WAIT FOR REVIEW

### **What happens next:**

1. **"In Review" status:** Amazon reviews your book
2. **Review time:** Usually 24-72 hours (sometimes faster)
3. **You'll get an email** when your book is live
4. **Status changes to "Live"** on your KDP dashboard

### **While waiting:**

- Check your email for confirmation
- Monitor your KDP dashboard
- Prepare your marketing plan
- Set up social media posts
- Tell friends and family

---

## 🚨 POSSIBLE ISSUES DURING REVIEW

### **If your book is rejected:**

Amazon will email you with the reason. Common issues:

**1. Content Quality Issues**
- Solution: Fix formatting, spelling, grammar errors
- Re-upload manuscript

**2. Cover Issues**
- Solution: Cover too small, wrong format, poor quality
- Re-upload higher quality cover

**3. Medical Content Flagged**
- Solution: Ensure strong disclaimer is present
- May require additional review

**4. Copyright Issues**
- Solution: Verify you own all rights to content
- Provide documentation if requested

**5. Metadata Issues**
- Solution: Title, description, or keywords violate terms
- Revise and resubmit

**Fix the issue and re-submit.** Amazon usually reviews resubmissions quickly.

## 🛠️ TROUBLESHOOTING

### **Book not showing up in search:**

- Wait 24-72 hours for Amazon's indexing
- Check if book is actually "Live" in KDP
- Search by ASIN instead of title
- Clear your browser cache

### **Cover looks wrong:**

- Check file dimensions (should be 2560×1600)
- Verify file format (JPEG or TIFF)
- Re-upload if necessary

### **Formatting issues in preview:**

- Fix source Word document
- Re-upload manuscript
- Use "Preview online" to check changes

### **Not getting sales:**

- Improve book description
- Get more reviews (minimum 5-10)
- Try Amazon Ads
- Lower price temporarily
- Use KDP Select free promotion days
