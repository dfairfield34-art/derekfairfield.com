## 🚀 QUICK START GUIDE

### **To Publish Your Book in 3 Steps:**

#### **STEP 1: Go to KDP**
1. Visit https://kdp.amazon.com
2. Sign in with your Amazon account
3. Click "+ Create" → "Kindle eBook"

#### **STEP 2: Upload Files**
1. **Page 1 (Details):**
   - Copy title, description, keywords from `AMAZON-LISTING.md`
   - Select categories as recommended
2. **Page 2 (Content):**
   - Upload `liver-health-guide-KDP.docx` as manuscript
   - Upload `liver-health-cover.jpg` as cover
   - Preview your book
3. **Page 3 (Pricing):**
   - Set price: **$7.99** (recommended)
   - Choose **70% royalty**
   - Select worldwide territories

#### **STEP 3: Publish**
1. Review all information
2. Click "Publish Your Kindle eBook"
3. Wait 24-72 hours for Amazon review
4. Your book goes live!

**For detailed instructions, see:** `KDP-PUBLISHING-CHECKLIST.md`
